FROM READING TO REMEMBERING

Make every page
part of your practice.

Build your vocabulary from what you actually read.

  1. 01 / READ

    Follow your interest.

    Keep the original English in view. Translate one passage when you need help, or read English and Chinese side by side.

  2. 02 / COLLECT

    Keep the context.

    Look up words and phrases offline in the extension. Save the meaning you need, along with the original sentence and source.

  3. 03 / REMEMBER

    Come back to it.

    Recall a meaning before revealing it. Short, spaced reviews bring your saved words back when they are due.

Your Mac does the translating.
English-to-Chinese translation uses a local model on an Apple Silicon Mac. No cloud translation API key required.
Your vocabulary stays portable.
The learning center saves to this browser. Export a JSON backup or take your words to Anki whenever you like.
Sync is your choice.
Connect your own server to share vocabulary and review progress across devices. Web lookup uses the server’s dictionary; offline extension lookup requires a dictionary download.
